Account Security & Maintenance Policy

To protect the integrity of all customer assets, our platform uses a secure asset protection system. Customer funds remain under the customer's control and cannot be accessed, modified, or deducted by our company unless authorized through approved processes.

Inactive Account Protection: If an account has no active investment plan and the account holder does not log in for 90 consecutive days (3 months), available funds enter Protected Lock Status.

Monthly Maintenance Fee: Accounts in Protected Lock Status incur a fixed $4.00/month maintenance fee covering monitoring, asset protection infrastructure, and compliance.

Unlocking: Depositing the calculated maintenance amount automatically clears the outstanding balance and restores complete access immediately.
